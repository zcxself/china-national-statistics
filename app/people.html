People · 2024

1,408.28 million people — and fewer every year

The population fell by 1.39 million in 2024, the third annual fall in a row. At the same time the country kept urbanising — 67.00% now live in towns and cities — and kept ageing: 310.31 million people are 60 or older.

Births and deaths, 2021–2024

Millions per year. Deaths have outnumbered births since 2022.

Births rose in 2024 — to 9.54 million from 9.02 million in 2023 — the first rise since 2016, but deaths (10.93m) still exceeded them.

Source: NBS communiques 2021–2024.

Urbanisation, 2015–2024

Share of permanent residents living in urban areas, %.

The urban share rose 0.84 points in 2024 alone. In 2015 it was 56.10% — an 11-point rise in a decade.

Source: NBS communiques 2015–2024.

Ageing: the 300-million milestone

In 2024 the number of people aged 60 and over reached 310.31 million (22.0% of the population) — the first time it has passed 300 million. Those aged 65 and over number 220.35 million (15.6%). The working-age population (16–59) is 857.88 million, 60.9% of the total.
