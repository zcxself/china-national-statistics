China in 2024: still growing, still ageing, more urban than ever

GDP rose 5.0% to $18.73 trillion. The population fell for a third year, to 1,408.28 million, while the urban share reached 67.00% and the number of people aged 60 and over passed 300 million for the first time.

How to read this site. Every figure carries its source and year. Headline numbers come from China’s National Bureau of Statistics (NBS) 2024 statistical communique and the World Bank’s World Development Indicators (July 2026 update). Where the two disagree on a definition — year-end residents versus mid-year estimates — the Sources page says so.

Population, 2015–2024

Mid-year estimates; the headline 1,408.28m is the NBS year-end count.

Population peaked in 2021 at 1,412.4 million (mid-year estimate) and has fallen every year since — by 1.39 million in 2024 alone.

Source: World Bank WDI; NBS 2024 communique. More on people →

The number that defines the decade: 310.31 million

For the first time, more than 300 million people in China are aged 60 or over — 22.0% of the population. At the same time, 9.54 million babies were born and 10.93 million people died: a natural decrease of 0.99 per 1,000. See People for births, deaths and the age structure.
