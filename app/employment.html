Employment · 2024

734.39 million people at work — and 5.1% looking

Employment held up in 2024: 12.56 million new jobs appeared in towns and cities, and the surveyed urban unemployment rate averaged 5.1% for the year. Nearly half of all workers are now in services.

Employment in millions

Migrant workers are counted inside the employed total, not in addition to it.

Almost two in three workers (64.5%) now work in towns and cities, up with the urbanisation rate of 67.00%.

Source: NBS 2024 communique.

  • Working away from home178.71m

  • Change on 2023+0.7%

Migrant workers are people with a rural household registration who work away from their home village or town. The two groups above add to the 299.73 million total.

Source: NBS 2024 communique.

What the unemployment rate measures

The 5.1% is the surveyed urban unemployment rate — a monthly survey of people in towns and cities, not a count of registered jobseekers. It averaged 5.1% across 2024 and stood at 5.1% in December, so the labour market ended the year about where it started it.

Source: NBS 2024 communique.
