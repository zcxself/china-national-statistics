Trade · 2024

¥43.85 trillion of goods crossed the border — a record

Goods trade grew 5.0% in 2024 to a new high, and China stayed the world’s largest goods trader for an eighth straight year. Exports grew faster than imports, so the surplus widened.

A record year, in a row of records

Total goods trade of ¥43.85tn was the highest ever recorded, and 2024 was the eighth consecutive year China led the world in goods trade. Exports grew 7.1% while imports grew 2.3% — the gap between the two is what widened the surplus.

Reading the numbers

  • Goods only. These figures cover trade in goods; trade in services is counted separately and is not included here.
  • Rounding. Exports (¥25.45tn) and imports (¥18.39tn) sum to ¥43.84tn; the published total is ¥43.85tn. The ¥0.01tn difference is rounding in the published components.
  • Dollars are derived. The yuan figures are the published ones; dollar figures divide them by the 2024 average exchange rate of ¥7.1217 per US$.
  • Why it matters for growth. Net exports contributed 1.5 percentage points of the 5.0% GDP growth in 2024 — see Economy.

Source: General Administration of Customs, 2024 (via Xinhua, 14 Jan 2025); NBS 2024 communique for the growth contribution.
