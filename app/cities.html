Cities · 2024

Ten cities carry the economy

Shanghai’s economy (¥53,927bn) is bigger than most countries’. The ten largest cities produced ¥315,503bn between them in 2024 — and 27 Chinese cities passed ¥1 trillion. But size and wealth are different things: Chongqing is the biggest city by people and the poorest of the ten per person, while Beijing earns the most per resident.

What the cities show

  • Two leagues: Shanghai (¥53,927bn) and Beijing (¥49,843bn) are a class of their own; the next eight run from Shenzhen (¥36,802bn) down to Nanjing (¥18,501bn).
  • Growth: Suzhou grew fastest of the ten (6.0%), then Shenzhen (5.8%); Guangzhou was slowest (2.1%), held back by its car industry.
  • People: Chongqing (31.91m) is the only city above 30 million; Shanghai (24.80m) actually shrank by 72,000 people in 2024, while Shenzhen added 199,000 — the most of any city.
  • Wealth per person: Beijing (¥228,303) and Shanghai (¥217,424) are more than twice Chongqing (¥100,874) — the municipal area counts farmland as well as the city itself.
