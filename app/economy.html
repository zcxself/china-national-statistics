Economy · 2024

A $18.73 trillion economy that grew 5.0%

GDP was ¥1,349,084 billion in 2024 — $18.73 trillion at market exchange rates, $38.19 trillion on a purchasing-power basis. Growth picked up through the year, from 4.6% in the third quarter to 5.4% in the fourth.

GDP in current US dollars, 2015–2024

The 2020–2021 jump is mostly the yuan’s appreciation against the dollar, not a surge in output — output grew 8.1% in real terms in 2021.

Source: World Bank, World Development Indicators (July 2026 update). Earlier editions of the same dataset gave $18.75tn for 2024; the July 2026 update revised it to $18.73tn.

What drove the 5.0%?

Contributions to 2024 growth, percentage points.

Consumption contributed the most (2.2 points), net exports 1.5 points and investment 1.3 points. The three sum to 5.0.

On a purchasing-power basis, China is No. 1

GDP (PPP), 2024, $ trillion. PPP adjusts for what money buys locally, so it suits comparing living standards; market exchange rates suit comparing trade and finance.

China’s GDP (PPP) was $38.19 trillion in 2024 — $27,105 per person — ahead of the United States at $29.30 trillion. On market exchange rates the order reverses: the US at $29.30tn, China at $18.73tn.

Source: World Bank WDI (July 2026 update). India’s PPP figure is approximate ($15.6tn).

Inside industry

  • Equipment manufacturing grew 7.7% in 2024 — 34.6% of value added by industrial firms above the designated size.
  • High-technology manufacturing grew 8.9% — 16.3% of the same total.
  • GDP per capita reached ¥95,749 ($13,293), up 5.1% in real terms on 2023.
  • Gross national income was ¥1,339,672 billion, up 5.1%.

Source: NBS 2024 communique (English edition, 28 Feb 2025).
