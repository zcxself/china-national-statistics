Energy · 2024

Self-sufficient, cleaner per yuan — and building 13.17 million electric cars

China produced more than 80% of the energy it used in 2024, and each yuan of GDP took less energy and less carbon to make than the year before. The year’s standout figure is industrial: 13.17 million new-energy vehicles rolled off production lines, the first time any country has passed ten million in a year.

What “self-sufficiency above 80%” means

China produced more than four-fifths of the energy it consumed in 2024 from its own coal, oil, gas, hydro, nuclear, wind and solar capacity, and imported the rest. The rate has held above 80% for years: domestic production, not imports, covers most of what the economy burns — and the share of non-fossil energy keeps rising inside that total.

Source: NBS 2024 communique.

A note on scope

This page carries only the energy figures the 2024 communique publishes in a form that can be quoted exactly: the self-sufficiency rate, the two intensity changes, and new-energy vehicle output. Totals such as all-society electricity consumption are not in this release in a comparable form, so this page does not estimate them.
