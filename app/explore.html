What to look for

  • GDP: up 66.0% in dollars from 2015 to 2024 — but the 2020–2021 jump is mostly the yuan’s appreciation, not a surge in output.
  • GDP per capita: $8,175 in 2015 to $13,293 in 2024, up 62.6%; still below Japan ($33,797) and the US ($86,170).
  • Population: peaked at 1,412.4 million in 2021 (mid-year estimate) and has fallen every year since.
  • Urbanisation: 56.1% in 2015 to 67.0% in 2024 — two in three residents now live in towns and cities.
  • Births & deaths: deaths have outnumbered births every year since 2022; 2024 saw 9.54 million births against 10.93 million deaths.

Sources: World Bank WDI (July 2026 update); NBS communiques 2021–2024. See Sources for the exact publications.
